Hello! I'm Yaregal Semanew

A passionate Neonatal Nursing educator, researcher, and innovator based in the Amhara region of Ethiopia.

For the past six years, I have been serving as a lecturer at Wollo University, where I teach neonatal nursing courses and mentor the next generation of healthcare professionals. Before joining academia, I worked in Neonatal Intensive Care Units (NICUs) at hospitals across the Amhara region, gaining hands-on experience in caring for the most vulnerable patients.

My research focuses on improving neonatal outcomes, and I have published my work in reputable journals. I am also deeply interested in leveraging modern technology to enhance teaching and learning — currently developing a custom Learning Management System (LMS) to help students learn remotely and access quality education anytime, anywhere.
